One jacket, passed from winner to winner — like the green jacket at the Masters. A few things are still open. Tap the options below; the jacket at the bottom changes as you tap. Then copy your picks and send them to Lachlan.

Already decided
  • The jacket: navy with a pinstripe.
  • Front: the club crest on the left chest — DLG, with INVITATIONAL right across underneath.

Notes for the embroiderer (for later)
JacketOne jacket, passed from winner to winner (like the Masters green jacket). Navy pinstripe jacket (already chosen). Order a size the likely winners can all get into.
Front — left chestThe only crest on the jacket. The club crest emblem — 100 mm wide. Wearer's left chest, about 18–20 cm down from the shoulder seam, centred between the zip and the side seam. Wording: "EST ★ 2026" above the shield · DLG · INVITATIONAL · scroll reads "GOOD MATES · VITA BREVIS · BAD GOLF".
Back — bannerRibbon reading "GOOD MATES · VITA BREVIS · BAD GOLF" — 400 mm wide, centred across the top of the back, top edge about 6 cm below the collar seam. Same colours as the crest.
Back — roll of honourNo crest on the back. Directly under the banner: "ROLL OF HONOUR" heading (about 22 mm letters) with a gold rule under it, then one line per year: year · course · winner. Line letters about 15–18 mm high, block about 360 mm wide, centred. Stitch now: "2026 · MISSION HILLS" only, with a clear gap after it on the same line for the winner's name, which goes on after the comp (Fri 4 Dec 2026). Nothing else on the roll is stitched yet — the dotted lines on the mockup just show the room left for ten winners in all (to 2035). The jacket comes back each year to have the new winner's line stitched on, so keep the digitised file.
Thread coloursGold · White (the shield and ribbon are navy, the same as the jacket) — flat fill, no gradients. Reference colours: gold #C9A227 · white · navy #1B2A41 to match the jacket. Match to the nearest stock thread.
LetteringCopperplate-style capitals. INVITATIONAL is stretched to exactly the width of DLG above it. On the chest the small words (INVITATIONAL, EST 2026, VITA BREVIS) come out about 4–5 mm tall — the full slogan in the scroll comes out about 2 mm, which most embroiderers can't stitch cleanly — if that's too small for clean stitching, the chest crest can go up to 110 mm, or drop EST · 2026 from the chest version only.
